A hidden rule maps a list of 8 digits to another list of 8 digits.
The rule applies at most 10 operations from this library, in order.
The same operation can occur more than once.

- pairswap: swap the elements at positions 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 and 7-8.
- complement: replace every element x with 9 - x.
- prefix: replace every element with the sum of it and all elements before it, modulo 10.
- rotate c: move the first c elements to the end, in the same order. c is 1 to 7.
- add c: add c to every element, modulo 10. c is 1 to 9.
- addindex: add its position to every element, modulo 10. The first position is 0.
- reverse: reverse the order of the list.
- mul c: multiply every element by c, modulo 10. c is 3, 7 or 9.
- sort: sort the list in ascending order.

Examples:
[5 9 0 5 1 0 0 2] -> [5 4 4 8 5 4 3 1]
[6 6 2 5 6 6 4 8] -> [0 5 5 9 5 2 8 1]
[2 9 4 7 6 7 8 1] -> [8 9 1 0 6 1 1 4]
[8 0 0 6 9 1 4 8] -> [3 8 7 2 9 8 6 6]
[7 8 1 6 5 8 5 7] -> [5 9 6 3 7 5 0 4]
[7 2 8 3 2 4 8 3] -> [9 0 6 3 8 9 5 2]

Find a rule of at most 10 operations that reproduces every example.
Any rule that reproduces every example is correct.
